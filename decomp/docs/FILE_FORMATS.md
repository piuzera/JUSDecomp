## Format detail

### `ALAR` — AAR archive (317 files; dominant format) — confirmed magic

Header sample (`chr/ChrArc.aar`): `41 4C 41 52 03 45 15 03 …` ("ALAR", then version/count
fields). Known from JUSToolkit research to be JUS's compound archive containing NCGR/NCLR/
NCER/NANR-like sub-objects (graphics, palettes, cell/animation data). Full layout
**tentative** until we document offsets/headers ourselves.

### `ALTM` + `DSIG` — UI/layout pairs — confirmed magics

`.atm` (ALTM, 6 files) and `.dig` (DSIG, 2 files) appear as pairs (e.g. `battle/deck00.atm` +
`battle/deck00.dig`, `Common/win_c00.atm` + `Common/win_c00.dig`). JUSToolkit documents
AT+DIG as the JUS screen/window layout formats. Internal layout **tentative**.

### `DSCP` — unknown container — confirmed magic, meaning tentative

10 files total carry `DSCP` (2 `.aar`, 4 `.atm`, 4 `.dig`). High-priority identification
target during UI-format analysis.

### `ALFT` — font archives — confirmed magic

`font/DSFont.aft`, `js8font.aft`, `jskfont.aft`, `jskfont_q.aft`. Likely NCGR+width-map
containers; **tentative**.

### Standard Nitro formats — confirmed

- `NCCL` palette (`Commu/com_bg.ncl`, `JArena/arena_bg.ncl`)
- `SDAT` sound archive (`sound/JS2_sound.sdat`) — NitroSDK standard, well documented publicly.
- `.char`/`.plt` Nitro character/palette for the download-play icon.

### Raw `.bin` data tables — confirmed existence, contents tentative

`bin/` holds gameplay-relevant tables whose names indicate purpose:
`ability(.bin/_t.bin)`, `bgm.bin`, `chr_b(.bin/_t)`, `chr_s(.bin/_t)`, `koma.bin`,
`komatxt.bin`, `kshape.bin`, `stage.bin`, `state.bin`, `pname.bin`, `infoname.bin`, etc.
These are high-value early targets for structure reconstruction (koma/deck parameters).

### Special files

- `ChildRom/JSS2Child.srl` — a full second NDS ROM embedded for DS Download Play
  (multiplayer without a game card). Internal title reads `JUS\0` ("JSS2Child" =
  Jump Super Stars 2 child). Note: this is original copyrighted data; never commit.
- `dwc/utility.bin` (~0.86 MB) — DWC utility binary for Wi-Fi Connection.
- `opening/opening.vx` — Actimagine VX-encoded opening video, magic `VXDS` (codec present in
  ARM9: `[SDK+Actimagine:VX]`).
